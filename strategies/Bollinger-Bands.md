## Bollinger Bands

## Prerequisites
* [Simple Moving Average ](../theory/moving-average.md)
* [Standard Deviation](../theory/Standard-Dev.md)


## What are they?

They are not a trading strategy by them selves but rather they are a measurement or gauge used by other strategies to tell them about the assets volatility.

## The picture

You take a 20 day SMA as your base approx. for the assets price and it will look like a line compared to the asset. 
If you can imagine that then we are basically done because the "bands" are just that same curve / line but 2 $\sigma$ or sd both above and below.

## The idea

When things become messy, the bands expand which tells us that the sd has increased hence the volatility has. And vise versa for when they contract there would be what we consider "low" volatility.

## Why?

If you could put a number on how volatile an asset is then you would be rich and wouldn't need to read this, but this is why methods like boll bands are for.

They give us an approx. using statistics

## Note limitations

* Can assume distributions depending on where you wish to apply it
* It is an approx. dont get caught up in these fax


## Example uses for trading

* If bands expand you lower your inventory.
* If bands contract can assume will expand at some point and might take advantage of that fact( kind of like kinetic energy building up ).
* Used for risk management.
