## What is it?

Simply having a good algorithm or trading strategy is not enough to make it big.
Knowing how to diversify, manage risk and how much to invest are all things one must consider when optimizing their portfolio.

$$\therefore$$ this section will aim to cover the things we have researched and plan to implement in future portfolios


This is an evolving framework and the following are what we will look into:

Asset Allocation: Determining optimal weightings for a basket of assets.

Risk Metrics: Quantifying exposure and potential downside (e.g., VaR, CVaR).

Systemic Constraints: Accounting for transaction costs, liquidity, and leverage.
